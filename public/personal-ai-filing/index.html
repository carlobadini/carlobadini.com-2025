personal-ai-filing

A private document filing tool used only by its owner, Carlo Badini. It is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

What it does

personal-ai-filing finds important documents (contracts, invoices, receipts, tax documents, statements, insurance papers) across the owner's own email accounts, cloud drives, notes and local folders. It organizes them into a searchable archive on the owner's own computer, so they can be found by describing their contents.

How it uses Google data

  • Gmail (read-only): reads messages that have attachments, and emails that are documents themselves (such as receipts and booking confirmations), to find and archive important documents.
  • Google Drive (read-only): indexes files in folders the owner has selected, so they can be searched. Files stay where they are.
  • The tool never sends email, never changes, moves, renames or deletes anything, and never changes sharing settings. It only requests the read-only scopes gmail.readonly and drive.readonly.
